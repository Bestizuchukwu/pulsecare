A flexible day of balanced meals

Use these meal-time examples as a starting point. There is no single best schedule for everyone—fit meals around your sleep, routine, culture, appetite, and health needs.

General wellness information only, not a prescribed diet or a promise of weight or fitness results. For allergies, pregnancy, medical conditions, medication-related food restrictions, or specific nutrition goals, ask a qualified health professional or registered dietitian.

Example day

Meals & suggested times

🌅7:00–9:00 AM

Breakfast

Try oats or whole-grain toast with eggs, yogurt, beans, or nut butter, plus fruit.

Why it helps: A mix of fiber and protein can make a satisfying start and contribute useful nutrients.

🍎10:00–11:00 AM

Optional snack

Choose fruit with yogurt, a small handful of nuts, or vegetables with hummus if you are hungry.

Why it helps: A convenient snack can bridge a long gap between meals; skip it if it does not suit your appetite.

☀️12:00–2:00 PM

Lunch

Build a bowl or plate with vegetables, a whole grain or starchy staple, and protein such as lentils, fish, tofu, chicken, or beans.

Why it helps: Combining food groups offers variety and supports energy and nutrient intake through the day.

🥜3:00–4:30 PM

Optional afternoon snack

Consider whole-grain crackers with cheese, fruit with nut butter, or roasted chickpeas if you want something between meals.

Why it helps: A planned option may be practical on busy days; snacks are not required for everyone.

🌙6:00–8:00 PM

Dinner

Try vegetables with a protein food and a satisfying staple—for example, beans and rice, tofu with noodles, or fish with potatoes.

Why it helps: A varied evening meal can round out the day; choose timing and portions that feel comfortable for you.

A simple guide

Make it balanced, your way

  • 🥦Add colorInclude vegetables or fruit when available; fresh, frozen, and canned options can all work.
  • 🌾Choose filling staplesWhole grains, potatoes, rice, maize, or other familiar staples can fit a balanced meal.
  • 🫘Include proteinBeans, lentils, eggs, dairy, fish, tofu, nuts, or meat are possible choices.
  • 💧Drink to thirstKeep water available if it suits you; fluid needs differ with health, activity, and climate.
